What is the structural difference between Internal and External Fragmentation in fixed-size paging systems?

Consider physical page frames vs. logical segment allocations and whether unused space resides within allocated boundaries or dispersed between them.

verified Grounded in: Lecture 07: Virtual Memory Invariants.pdf (Page 12, Theorem 3)
Click to reveal solution flip
SOLUTION & ARCHITECTURE Page Frames (Fixed 4KB)
Internal Prevalent in Paging

Unused storage inside allocated fixed memory blocks. When a process requests 5KB with 4KB pages, two frames (8KB) are given; 3KB remains wasted inside the second frame.

Loss = (Allocated Frame × N) - Requested Size
External Eliminated by Paging

Total free memory exists to satisfy a request, but storage is fragmented into non-contiguous slices. Pure paging eliminates this because physical frames do not need contiguous mapping.

Paging TLB resolves disparate physical pages
